HYSA Interest Calculator

Calculate the interest earned on a High Yield Savings Account.

How are these numbers calculated?

Monthly Compound Interest

This calculator uses monthly compounding, which means interest is calculated and added to your balance once per month.

Step-by-Step Calculation

  1. Convert annual rate to monthly rate:
    Monthly Rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12
    (e.g., 4.5% annual = 0.375% monthly)
  2. Each month, calculate interest earned:
    Interest = Current Balance × Monthly Rate
  3. Add interest to balance:
    New Balance = Current Balance + Interest
  4. Repeat for next month using the new balance (this is why it's called "compound" interest—you earn interest on your interest)
Example: $10,000 at 4.5% APY
  • Monthly rate: 4.5% ÷ 12 = 0.375% = 0.00375
  • Month 1: $10,000 × 0.00375 = $37.50 interest
  • New balance: $10,037.50
  • Month 2: $10,037.50 × 0.00375 = $37.64 interest
  • New balance: $10,075.14
  • ...and so on

Notice Month 2 earns $0.14 more than Month 1—that's compound interest at work!
